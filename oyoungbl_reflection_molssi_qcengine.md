# Reflection: molssi_qcengine

## Inactivity patterns
The project peaked early, with 951 commits in 2019 and 465 in 2020, then settled at a lower but stable level of roughly 200 to 260 commits a year from 2022 to 2025. This is a decline from a founding burst to a maintenance rhythm rather than a fade-out. It never had a gap longer than two months.

## Was the longest gap easy or hard to interpret?
Easy. Just before the gap, commits cover the 0.23.0 release and CI fixes (Mar 2022). Right after it (Jun 2022), Lori Burns made a series of commits tuning the Psi4 environments "after v1.6 rel".

## Likely reasons for inactivity or recovery
There is no inactivity to explain. The pause seems tied to the release schedule of the quantum chemistry package it wraps. The most recent commits (Nov 2025) are CI and dependency updates by the main maintainer, which fits a mature project in maintenance mode.

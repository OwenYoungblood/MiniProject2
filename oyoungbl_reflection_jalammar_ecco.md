# Reflection: jalammar_ecco

**Project facts:** 349 commits, 31 authors, 2020-11 to 2025-09. Pattern: declining. Longest gap: 2022-04 to 2023-05 (14 months). Gaps of 3+ months: 3. Status: Inactive (last GitHub commit 2024-08-15).

## Inactivity patterns
Ecco was most active in its first two years (120 commits in 2020 and 166 in 2021), then fell to 25 in 2022, 20 in 2023, 15 in 2024 and 3 in 2025. The decline is clear and long term. Of the 349 commits, only 38 come after the longest gap, so the project never regained its early level.

## Was the longest gap easy or hard to interpret?
Fairly easy. The last commits by the original author are from Jan 2022 (BART settings, model-config, README), and GitHub showed no pull requests during the gap while issues kept being opened. Issue #85 has no comments, which suggests reports went unanswered. The harder question is why the maintainer stepped back, and nothing in the data answers that.

## Likely reasons for inactivity or recovery
My hypothesis is that a single maintainer moved on and the project was left to the community. Recovery came mostly from outside contributors (4 of 6 post-gap authors were new): model-config updates in Jun 2023, OPT support in Jul 2023 and fp16 support in Aug 2023, plus a CI fix from the original author on 2023-07-27. The revival was fragile. Issue #115, opened 2024-05-16, asks whether the project is still maintained, and the last commits (Sep 2025) are a requirements update and a notebook fix from a contributor.

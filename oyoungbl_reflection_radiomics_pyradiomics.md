# Reflection: radiomics_pyradiomics

**Project facts:** 1,679 commits, 58 authors, 2015-09 to 2025-10. Pattern: declining. Longest gap: 2021-05 to 2021-08 (4 months). Gaps of 3+ months: 2. Status: Active (last GitHub commit 2025-06-17, after the 2025-04-01 cutoff).

## Inactivity patterns
Activity peaked in 2016 and 2017 (459 and 439 commits) and decreased every year to a low of 8 commits in 2021, with 20 to 38 a year through 2024. The 225 commits in 2025 are not a real revival: the most recent commits are almost all Dependabot version bumps. The pattern is a long decline into maintenance mode.

## Was the longest gap easy or hard to interpret?
Moderate. The 4-month gap is short and sits in the low-activity stretch, so it is hard to call it a distinct event. GitHub also showed that issues kept being opened during the gap and nearly all were answered (only 2 went unanswered), so the project was still being looked after even though no code was committed.

## Likely reasons for inactivity or recovery
My hypothesis is that after the v3.0.1 release (Oct 2020) the core maintainers had little left to add, so commits tapered off. The gap ended with an outside contribution: PR #724 (pyradiomics-dcm mask-correction settings, merged 2021-10-01) and PR #734 (usage docs, merged 2021-12-14), from a mix of new and returning authors. I did not find any statement from the maintainers about why development slowed.

# Reflection: skuschel_postpic

## Inactivity patterns
Postpic peaked in 2017 (526 commits), then dropped to 112 in 2018 and 137 in 2019, and has been at about 10 to 50 commits a year since 2020. Seven gaps of three months or more show how thin the activity is. Most work comes from one maintainer, so the timeline follows his available time.

## Was the longest gap easy or hard to interpret?
Fairly easy. GitHub showed PR #268 in Oct 2022 and then nothing until PRs #269 to #272 in Sep to Dec 2023, so the project was fully idle in between.

## Likely reasons for inactivity or recovery
My hypothesis is that the maintainer had other commitments and returned when dependencies broke. Recovery was by the same person (Stephan Kuschel). A new contributor did add features in Mar 2025 (an HDF5 export), so the project is not dependent on one person alone.
